## Findings a learner should know

- **SJSU:** CS 46A (Introduction to Programming) is matched by Ohlone **CS-116**, not CS-102; CS 46B by CS-124; MATH 42 by CS-113; MATH 39 by MATH-103. All five SJSU impaction courses for Data Science have an Ohlone match (CS 46B or CS 22A: CS-124, or CS-142/CNET-142).
- **CSU East Bay:** only CS 100 is matched (by CS-102). DATA 105, DATA 130, DATA 205 and STAT 215 have **no Ohlone course articulated** in 2026–2027 — they would be taken after transfer unless CSU East Bay advises otherwise.
- **UC Davis:** calculus articulates **as sequences only** (MATH-101A + 101B; 101B + 101C); STA 035A–C and ECS 032B have no Ohlone match; ECS 020, 036A, 036C are matched by CS-113, CS-102 and CS-124 (UC Davis notes these are not the usual courses for its Data Science majors).
- **UC Merced** (2025–2026 agreement): MATH 024 needs **both** MATH-103 and MATH-104.
- The Ohlone degree is an A.S., **not an AS-T**: no AS-T admission advantage applies.
- Supporting-course guidance on the site is provisional and per target (SJSU: CS-113, MATH-103; UC: MATH-101C, MATH-103); the A.S. needs two, transfer preparation may need more. Confirm with a counselor.
