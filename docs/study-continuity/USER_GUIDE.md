# Saving your place and picking up where you stopped

Formula Intelligence remembers where you are in a lesson, on this device, so you never have to hunt for it again.

## The short version

1. Open a lesson and study as usual.
2. Your place is saved as you go: a step you stay on for a moment is remembered automatically.
3. To mark a place deliberately, press **Save my place** in the bar at the top of the lesson. It confirms only once your place is actually stored: *"Saved on this device: Step 4 of 6 — Try it, today, 4:12 PM."*
4. Leave whenever you like: close the tab, turn off the computer, come back next week.
5. On **Home**, press **Resume where I stopped**. The lesson opens at the step you saved.

When you are done for the day, **Finish study session** saves your place and shows a short summary: the lesson, the step, how many questions you answered this session, and whether the lesson's checkpoint is done. From there you can return Home, continue now, or open the course outline.

## Your saved place, and your recent place

- **Your saved place** is the one you chose with *Save my place* (or *Finish study session*). It stays put until you save somewhere else or remove it. Opening other lessons in the meantime does not change it.
- **Your recent place** is where the app last saved for you automatically. If you have never pressed *Save my place*, Home offers **Continue recent lesson** with that step.
- Saving in a different lesson replaces your saved place. The steps panel tells you which lesson it will replace.

## In a lesson

- **Step N of 6** in the top bar opens the list of the six steps: *Why it matters, Understand, See it, Try it, Use it, Check understanding*. Each step is marked **You are here**, **Visited**, **Not reached** or **saved place**. Choose a step to go there.
- If you open a lesson from its course and you had a place deeper in it, the top of the lesson offers **Resume from Step N**.
- At the end of a lesson: **Recommended next**, **Review this lesson from Step 1**, **Save & finish for today**.

A visited step is a step you looked at, not a skill you have. Skills are judged only from your answers (My Progress → Your skills).

## On a course page

- **Your saved place** or **Where you were** shows the unit, lesson and step, with one button to go back to it. A place saved in another course never takes over this one.
- Three separate facts, never added up into one percentage:
  - **Written lessons**: how many have their checkpoint answered.
  - **Course content**: how much of the course is written yet.
  - **Skills shown in your answers**: how many of the course's exit skills are at proficient or better.
- Units show **In progress**, **Available lessons finished** or **Not started**. Lessons that are only planned show **Not written yet** and cannot be opened.
- When you have answered the checkpoint of every written lesson, the page says **You finished the available lessons in this course**. If more lessons are planned, it says the full course is not yet complete. It suggests the next written lesson in a course that builds on this one, and mixed review in Practice. There is no course-wide exam yet. This is a self-study app: nothing here is a grade, transcript or credential.

## In My Progress

**Your study journey** shows your saved place (with **Resume** and **Remove saved place**) and the courses you studied lately, each with where you were, written lessons checked, and skills. Removing your saved place removes only the bookmark. Your answers, checkpoints, notes and reviews stay.

## Where it is stored — please read

- Everything is stored **in this browser on this device** only. There is no account and no sync.
- Another browser, another device, or a different browser profile starts empty. To continue elsewhere, use **My Progress → Settings → Download backup**, then restore that file on the other device.
- A **private or incognito window** may delete everything when it closes.
- If your browser refuses to store data (storage full or blocked), the app says **"Couldn’t save to this browser"** and never claims your place was saved. Your place is then kept only until you close the tab.
